Our Motto

At Cola Primary School, we believe in the transformative power of education. Our emblem, featuring the open book and the radiant motto “Let There Be Light,” symbolizes our commitment to enlightening young minds with knowledge, values, and skills for a bright future. Like the shield that surrounds our school name, we are dedicated to providing a nurturing and safe environment where students can grow intellectually, socially, and emotionally.


Here, every child is guided toward discovering their potential, with a focus on integrity, curiosity, and community. We invite you to explore our site and learn more about our mission, our dedicated staff, and the opportunities we offer to help your child thrive!
